Software Features

SRL Khata provides the essential accounting tools needed to manage daily business transactions and reports.

Sale Management

Create sale vouchers, maintain customer accounts, calculate amounts and keep complete sale records.

Purchase Management

Record purchases, supplier transactions, quantities, rates and purchase-related accounting.

Voucher Management

Manage payment, receipt and other business voucher transactions in an organized manner.

Ledger

Maintain customer, supplier, cash, bank and other ledger accounts with proper debit and credit records.

Item Master

Maintain items, quantities, rates and stock-related information for day-to-day business operations.

Cash & Bank

Keep track of cash and bank transactions and review the financial position of the business.

Trading Account

Review purchase, sale and stock information through the trading account.

Profit & Loss

Review business income, expenses and profitability through the Profit & Loss report.

Balance Sheet

View the financial position of the business through the Balance Sheet.

Reports & Printing

View and print important accounting reports and previously recorded business data.
